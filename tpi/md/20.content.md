## Proverbs 1:1 (id: 10020001001)

1Dispela buk i gat ol gutpela toktok bilong Solomon, pikinini bilong Devit na king bilong Israel.


## Proverbs 1:2 (id: 10020001002)

2Dispela gutpela tok em inap long givim yumi gutpela tingting na long lainim yumi long gutpela pasin. Na sapos yumi harim tok long as bilong ol samting, orait bai yumi inap klia tru long dispela tok.


## Proverbs 1:3 (id: 10020001003)

3Gutpela tok bilong dispela buk bai i skulim yumi long wokabaut olsem man i gat gutpela save na long bihainim ol gutpela na stretpela pasin na long mekim wankain stretpela pasin long olgeta man.


## Proverbs 1:4 (id: 10020001004)

4Na sapos sampela man i gat liklik save tasol, bai dispela tok i helpim ol long kisim gutpela tingting, na em bai i givim gutpela save long ol yangpela man, na soim ol long gutpela pasin bilong wokabaut.


## Proverbs 1:5 (id: 10020001005)

5Na dispela buk bai i helpim ol man i gat save na bai ol i kisim save moa yet, na em bai i skulim ol man i gat gutpela tingting long ol gutpela rot ol i ken bihainim.


## Proverbs 1:6 (id: 10020001006)

6Dispela tok bai i helpim yumi long kisim save long ol tok piksa na tok bokis na long ol gutpela tok na tok hait bilong ol saveman.


## Proverbs 1:7 (id: 10020001007)

7Sapos yu laik kisim gutpela save, orait pastaim yu mas aninit long Bikpela. Ol man i no gat gutpela tingting ol i no laik tru long skul na kisim gutpela save.


## Proverbs 1:8 (id: 10020001008)

8Pikinini, yu mas harim tok bilong papamama bilong yu na yu no ken sakim tok bilong tupela.


## Proverbs 1:9 (id: 10020001009)

9Tok bilong tupela bai i mekim yu i kamap gutpela man, olsem yu putim naispela bilas long het bilong yu na naispela bis i stap long nek bilong yu.


## Proverbs 1:10 (id: 10020001010)

10Pikinini, sapos ol raskol o stilman i grisim yu long mekim ol pasin nogut, orait yu no ken bihainim tok bilong ol.


## Proverbs 1:11 (id: 10020001011)

11Dispela kain man i save tok olsem, “Goan, yu kam wantaim mipela, na yumi go kilim sampela man i dai. Yumi go hait long arere bilong rot na lukim wanem ol man bai i kam, na yumi kilim ol i dai. Maski ol i no gat asua, yumi pinisim laik tasol na kilim ol i dai.


## Proverbs 1:12 (id: 10020001012)

12Bai yumi kamap wantu na kilim ol, olsem dai i save kamap wantu long ol man na ol i go long ples bilong ol man i dai pinis.


## Proverbs 1:13 (id: 10020001013)

13Na bai yumi kisim planti samting bilong ol dispela man na bai ol dispela samting i pulap long ol haus bilong yumi.


## Proverbs 1:14 (id: 10020001014)

14Yu kam poroman wantaim mipela, na bai yu tu yu kisim planti samting.”


## Proverbs 1:15 (id: 10020001015)

15Pikinini, sapos ol raskol i mekim dispela kain tok long yu, orait yu no ken harim tok bilong ol na bihainim ol.


## Proverbs 1:16 (id: 10020001016)

16Oltaim ol dispela kain man i laikim tumas long mekim ol pasin nogut. Na oltaim bel bilong ol i save kirap long kilim ol man i dai.


## Proverbs 1:17-18 (id: 10020001017)

17\-18Ol man i mekim dispela kain pasin bai i kisim pe nogut. I olsem ol i putim umben bilong holimpas ol yet. Ol pisin i gat gutpela tingting. Sapos wanpela pisin i lukim man i putim umben, orait em bai i no inap pas long en. Tasol ol raskol i no olsem. Ol i strong moa long mekim pasin nogut, olsem na bai ol i bagarapim ol yet.


## Proverbs 1:19 (id: 10020001019)

19Ol man bilong pait na stil i save bihainim dispela pasin, olsem na ol bai i bagarap. I olsem ol samting ol i bin stilim bai i bagarapim ol.


## Proverbs 1:20 (id: 10020001020)

20Gutpela na stretpela tingting i olsem wanpela meri i sanap long ol rot na long ol maket na i singaut long ol manmeri.


## Proverbs 1:21 (id: 10020001021)

21Na tu em i sanap long ples bung klostu long dua bilong bikpela banis bilong taun, na long olgeta ples planti manmeri i stap long en, na em i singaut olsem,


## Proverbs 1:22 (id: 10020001022)

22“Yupela lain i no gat tingting, yupela i laik i stap olsem oltaim, a? Yupela i olsem ol longlong man. Yupela i no laik kisim gutpela save na yupela i tok bilas long en. Wanem taim bai yupela i lusim dispela pasin?


## Proverbs 1:23 (id: 10020001023)

23Mi laik tok strong long yupela long lusim pasin nogut bilong yupela. Yupela i mas bihainim tok bilong mi, na bai mi skulim yupela na givim gutpela tingting long yupela.


## Proverbs 1:24 (id: 10020001024)

24Mi bin singautim yupela long kam long mi, tasol yupela i no laik.


## Proverbs 1:25 (id: 10020001025)

25Yupela i bin givim baksait long gutpela tok bilong mi na yupela i bikhet long taim mi laik stretim yupela.


## Proverbs 1:26 (id: 10020001026)

26Orait bai mi tu mi mekim wankain pasin olsem yupela. Na long taim bikpela trabel i painim yupela, na yupela i bagarap, bai mi lap moa yet, na bai mi tok bilas long yupela.


## Proverbs 1:27 (id: 10020001027)

27Taim nogut bai i kamap long yupela olsem strongpela win, na trabel na pen bai i bagarapim yupela olsem traipela raunwin.


## Proverbs 1:28 (id: 10020001028)

28“Long dispela taim yupela bai i singautim mi long helpim yupela, tasol bai mi no ken i kam. Na yupela bai i wok long painim mi, tasol yupela bai i no inap lukim mi.


## Proverbs 1:29 (id: 10020001029)

29Yupela i no laik tru long kisim gutpela save na yupela i no laik aninit long Bikpela.


## Proverbs 1:30 (id: 10020001030)

30Yupela i bin sakim gutpela tok bilong mi, na yupela i bin givim baksait long mi long taim mi laik stretim yupela.


## Proverbs 1:31 (id: 10020001031)

31Olsem na nau bai yupela i kisim pe nogut bilong ol pasin nogut yupela i save mekim.


## Proverbs 1:32 (id: 10020001032)

32Ol manmeri i no gat gutpela tingting, ol i save givim baksait long mi, na dispela pasin bilong ol i save kilim ol yet i dai. Ol man i ting ol i save pinis long olgeta samting, ol i olsem ol longlong man. Dispela tingting bilong ol bai i bagarapim ol yet.


## Proverbs 1:33 (id: 10020001033)

33Tasol ol manmeri i putim yau gut long tok bilong mi, bai ol i stap gut. Na bai ol i no gat hevi na bai ol i no pret, long wanem, ol i save, samting nogut i no inap kamap long ol.”


## Proverbs 2:1 (id: 10020002001)

1Pikinini, yu mas harim tok bilong mi na putim gut long tingting bilong yu.


## Proverbs 2:2 (id: 10020002002)

2Yu mas putim yau gut long olgeta tok i save givim yu gutpela tingting. Na yu mas wok strong long kisim gutpela save.


## Proverbs 2:3 (id: 10020002003)

3Na yu mas askim gutpela tingting na save long kam long yu, olsem yu singautim wanpela man i kam bilong helpim yu.


## Proverbs 2:4 (id: 10020002004)

4Na yu mas wok hat long painim gutpela tingting, olsem man i wok long painim silva ol i bin haitim insait long graun na olsem man i wok long painim mani samting ol i bin haitim.


## Proverbs 2:5 (id: 10020002005)

5Sapos yu mekim ol dispela samting, orait bai yu save tru long pasin bilong stap aninit long Bikpela, na bai yu save gut long God.


## Proverbs 2:6 (id: 10020002006)

6Long wanem, Bikpela yet em i as bilong gutpela tingting. Na em i save givim gutpela tingting na save long yumi.


## Proverbs 2:7 (id: 10020002007)

7Em i save stiaim tingting bilong ol stretpela man na ol i save skelim gut ol pasin ol i mas bihainim. Na em i save lukautim ol gut na ol i no bagarap.


## Proverbs 2:8 (id: 10020002008)

8God i banisim ol man i save mekim stretpela pasin na em i was long rot bilong ol man i bihainim em.


## Proverbs 2:9 (id: 10020002009)

9Pikinini, sapos yu bihainim gutpela tingting bai yu save long olgeta gutpela rot bilong wokabaut. Bai yu save long bihainim ol gutpela na stretpela pasin na long mekim wankain pasin long olgeta man.


## Proverbs 2:10 (id: 10020002010)

10Bai yu kisim planti gutpela tingting na bai yu belgut long gutpela save bilong yu.


## Proverbs 2:11 (id: 10020002011)

11Pasin bilong tingting gut bai i banisim yu na gutpela save bai i was long yu.


## Proverbs 2:12 (id: 10020002012)

12Na bai yu no bihainim ol man i save mekim pasin nogut na i save mekim tok i no stret.


## Proverbs 2:13 (id: 10020002013)

13Ol dispela man i save lusim stretpela rot na i wokabaut long rot i tudak.


## Proverbs 2:14 (id: 10020002014)

14Ol i save amamas long mekim kain kain pasin nogut.


## Proverbs 2:15 (id: 10020002015)

15Pasin bilong ol dispela man i no stret tru.


## Proverbs 2:16 (id: 10020002016)

16Na sapos meri bilong narapela man i laik grisim yu long mekim pasin pamuk wantaim em, orait gutpela tingting bai i banisim yu, na bai yu no inap bihainim tok bilong dispela meri.


## Proverbs 2:17 (id: 10020002017)

17Dispela kain meri i save lusim man em i bin maritim long taim em i yangpela. Na em i no moa tingim dispela promis em i bin mekim long ai bilong God.


## Proverbs 2:18 (id: 10020002018)

18Sapos yu go wantaim dispela meri long haus bilong en, i olsem yu bihainim rot i go long ples bilong ol man i dai pinis.


## Proverbs 2:19 (id: 10020002019)

19Ol man i save go lukim em, ol i no inap i kam bek. Na ol i no inap bihainim rot bilong ol man i gat laip.


## Proverbs 2:20 (id: 10020002020)

20Sapos yu gat gutpela tingting, bai yu wokabaut long rot bilong ol gutpela man na bihainim pasin bilong ol stretpela man.


## Proverbs 2:21 (id: 10020002021)

21Ol gutpela stretpela man bai i stap gut oltaim long graun bilong yumi.


## Proverbs 2:22 (id: 10020002022)

22Tasol God bai i rausim ol man i save mekim pasin nogut na i no save bihainim em. Em bai i kamautim ol, olsem man i kamautim kaikai long gaden bilong en.


## Proverbs 3:1 (id: 10020003001)

1Pikinini, mi laik skulim yu nau, na yu no ken lusim tingting long dispela tok. Nogat. Oltaim yu mas tingim gut.


## Proverbs 3:2 (id: 10020003002)

2Sapos yu bihainim dispela tok bai yu stap longpela taim long graun na bai yu sindaun gut tru.


## Proverbs 3:3 (id: 10020003003)

3Yu no ken lusim pasin bilong givim bel bilong yu long ol arapela man na pasin bilong poroman tru long ol arapela man. Yu mas bihainim dispela pasin na em bai i kamap olsem gutpela bilas long nek bilong yu. Na yu mas putim gut long tingting bilong yu.


## Proverbs 3:4 (id: 10020003004)

4Sapos yu mekim olsem, orait God bai i laikim yu na ol man tu bai i laikim yu, na wok bilong yu bai i kamap gutpela.


## Proverbs 3:5 (id: 10020003005)

5Yu mas bilip strong long Bikpela. Yu no ken ting save bilong yu yet inap helpim yu.


## Proverbs 3:6 (id: 10020003006)

6Long olgeta samting yu mekim, yu mas tingting strong long Bikpela na yu mekim, na em bai i soim yu stretpela rot long bihainim.


## Proverbs 3:7 (id: 10020003007)

7Nogut yu ting, save bilong yu yet em i samting tru. Nogat. Yu mas aninit long Bikpela na givim baksait long pasin nogut.


## Proverbs 3:8 (id: 10020003008)

8Sapos yu mekim olsem, dispela pasin bai i helpim yu tru, olsem marasin i save helpim bodi bilong yu, na mekim yu i stap gut.


## Proverbs 3:9 (id: 10020003009)

9Yu mas kisim ol namba wan hap kaikai i mau long gaden bilong yu na givim long Bikpela olsem ofa, na dispela bai i givim biknem long em.


## Proverbs 3:10 (id: 10020003010)

10Sapos yu mekim olsem, orait Bikpela bai i mekim gaden bilong yu i karim planti kaikai moa. Na bai yu inap pulapim tru haus kaikai bilong yu, na bai tang wain bilong yu i pulap tru na hap wain i kapsait.


## Proverbs 3:11 (id: 10020003011)

11Pikinini, sapos Bikpela i mekim wanpela samting long yu bilong stretim yu, yu no ken kros na bel nogut.


## Proverbs 3:12 (id: 10020003012)

12Long wanem, Bikpela i save stretim ol man em i laikim tumas, olsem ol papa i save stretim ol pikinini ol i laikim tumas.


## Proverbs 3:13 (id: 10020003013)

13Man i kisim gutpela tingting na save, em i ken amamas tru,


## Proverbs 3:14 (id: 10020003014)

14long wanem, gutpela na stretpela tingting i winim tru silva na gol


## Proverbs 3:15 (id: 10020003015)

15na ol gutpela ston i dia tumas. Na tu em i winim olgeta arapela samting yu laikim tumas.


## Proverbs 3:16 (id: 10020003016)

16Gutpela tingting bai i helpim yu na yu inap i stap longpela taim long graun. Na em bai i givim biknem na planti samting long yu.


## Proverbs 3:17 (id: 10020003017)

17Gutpela tingting bai i mekim yu belgut oltaim na mekim sindaun bilong yu i kamap gutpela.


## Proverbs 3:18 (id: 10020003018)

18Gutpela tingting i olsem diwai bilong givim laip. Olsem na ol man i holimpas gutpela tingting, ol i gat laip i gutpela tru na ol i amamas.


## Proverbs 3:19 (id: 10020003019)

19Long gutpela tingting na save bilong Bikpela yet, na em i bin wokim graun na skai


## Proverbs 3:20 (id: 10020003020)

20na em i save mekim wara i kamap long graun na em i save mekim ol klaut i bung na ren i pundaun.


## Proverbs 3:21 (id: 10020003021)

21Pikinini, yu mas was gut na holim strong gutpela tingting na save bilong yu. Nogut yu lusim.


## Proverbs 3:22 (id: 10020003022)

22Gutpela tingting na save bai i givim laip long yu na em bai i stap olsem gutpela bilas bilong yu.


## Proverbs 3:23 (id: 10020003023)

23Na bai yu wokabaut gut long rot na bai i no gat wanpela samting i sutim lek bilong yu.


## Proverbs 3:24 (id: 10020003024)

24Na long taim bilong slip bai yu no inap pret. Bai yu slip gut tru.


## Proverbs 3:25-26 (id: 10020003025)

25\-26Yu mas bilip strong long Bikpela na em bai i banisim yu na lukautim yu na ol birua bai i no inap holimpas yu long umben bilong ol. Olsem na sapos taim nogut i kamap long ol man nogut na ol i bagarap, yu no ken pret. Dispela kain taim i save kamap wantu long ol, olsem bikpela win na ren.


## Proverbs 3:27 (id: 10020003027)

27Sapos wanpela man i sot long samting na em i askim yu long givim em, yu no ken tok, “Nogat.” Sapos yu inap, yu mas givim em.


## Proverbs 3:28 (id: 10020003028)

28Sapos yu inap helpim wantok long taim em i askim yu, orait yu no ken tokim em olsem, “Wet pastaim. Tumora yu kam bek na bai mi givim yu.”


## Proverbs 3:29 (id: 10020003029)

29Ol wantok bilong yu i stap klostu long yu na ol i ting yu pren bilong ol. Olsem na yu no ken tingting long mekim nogut long ol.


## Proverbs 3:30 (id: 10020003030)

30Yu no ken tok pait nating wantaim man i no bin mekim nogut long yu.


## Proverbs 3:31 (id: 10020003031)

31Bel bilong yu i no ken kirap long bihainim pasin bilong ol man bilong pait, na yu no ken wokabaut long rot bilong ol.


## Proverbs 3:32 (id: 10020003032)

32Long wanem, Bikpela yet i no laik tru long kain man olsem. Tasol Bikpela i stap wanbel tru wantaim ol man i save wokabaut stret.


## Proverbs 3:33 (id: 10020003033)

33Bikpela i tok, ol man nogut bai i bagarap wantaim ol famili bilong ol. Tasol Bikpela i save mekim gut long ol stretpela man wantaim ol famili bilong ol, na em i lukautim ol.


## Proverbs 3:34 (id: 10020003034)

34Sapos ol man i tok bilas long ol arapela man, orait God bai i bekim wankain pasin long ol. Tasol em i save mekim gut long ol man i daunim ol yet.


## Proverbs 3:35 (id: 10020003035)

35Ol manmeri bai i givim biknem long ol man i gat gutpela tingting. Tasol ol man i no gat gutpela tingting i save bagarapim nem bilong ol yet.


## Proverbs 4:1 (id: 10020004001)

1Ol pikinini, yupela i mas harim gut tok bilong mi, papa bilong yupela, na bai yupela i kisim gutpela tingting.


## Proverbs 4:2 (id: 10020004002)

2Tok mi givim yupela, em i gutpela tru, na yupela i no ken tromoi i go.


## Proverbs 4:3 (id: 10020004003)

3Mama bilong mi i no bin karim narapela pikinini, mi wanpela tasol, na em i laikim mi tumas.


## Proverbs 4:4 (id: 10020004004)

4Na taim mi mangi yet, papa bilong mi i bin skulim mi. Em i tokim mi olsem, “Putim gut tok bilong mi long tingting bilong yu na bihainim, na bai yu stap gut olgeta.


## Proverbs 4:5 (id: 10020004005)

5Yu mas kisim gutpela tingting na gutpela save. Yu no ken sakim tok bilong mi o lusim tingting long en.


## Proverbs 4:6 (id: 10020004006)

6Yu no ken lusim gutpela tingting. Nogat. Yu mas laikim em tumas na bai em i lukautim yu gut.


## Proverbs 4:7 (id: 10020004007)

7Nambawan samting yu mas kisim, em i gutpela tingting. Tru, yu ken kisim planti arapela samting, tasol gutpela tingting em i nambawan tru.


## Proverbs 4:8 (id: 10020004008)

8Yu mas laikim gutpela tingting na holim strong, na em bai i givim biknem long yu.


## Proverbs 4:9 (id: 10020004009)

9Em i kamap olsem naispela bilas long het bilong yu na olsem gutpela hat king.”


## Proverbs 4:10 (id: 10020004010)

10Pikinini, yu mas harim gut tok bilong mi, na bai yu stap longpela taim long graun.


## Proverbs 4:11 (id: 10020004011)

11Mi save skulim yu long bihainim gutpela tingting na mi save stiaim yu long wokabaut long stretpela rot.


## Proverbs 4:12 (id: 10020004012)

12Sapos yu wokabaut long dispela rot, bai i no gat wanpela samting i pasim yu, na bai yu no inap pundaun.


## Proverbs 4:13 (id: 10020004013)

13Oltaim yu mas holimpas dispela gutpela tok bilong mi. Em i olsem as bilong laip bilong yu, olsem na yu mas lukautim gut.


## Proverbs 4:14 (id: 10020004014)

14Yu no ken wokabaut long rot bilong ol man nogut, na yu no ken bihainim pasin bilong ol.


## Proverbs 4:15 (id: 10020004015)

15Yu mas abrusim tru dispela rot na yu mas i go i stap longwe long en.


## Proverbs 4:16 (id: 10020004016)

16Sapos ol man nogut i no bin mekim samting nogut yet, orait ol i no inap slip. Sapos ol i mekim nogut long ol arapela man pastaim, orait ol inap slip gut.


## Proverbs 4:17 (id: 10020004017)

17Pasin nogut na pasin bilong bagarapim ol arapela man em i olsem kaikai na dring bilong ol.


## Proverbs 4:18-19 (id: 10020004018)

18\-19Rot bilong ol man nogut i tudak olsem biknait. Ol i save pundaun na ol i no save wanem samting i sutim lek bilong ol. Tasol rot bilong ol stretpela man i lait olsem san i kamap long moningtaim, na lait i wok long kamap strong moa, inap long belo.


## Proverbs 4:20 (id: 10020004020)

20Pikinini, putim yau gut long tok bilong mi.


## Proverbs 4:21 (id: 10020004021)

21Yu mas holimpas dispela tok bilong mi, na putim gut long tingting bilong yu. Nogut yu lusim.


## Proverbs 4:22 (id: 10020004022)

22Sapos yu kisim tok bilong mi, orait dispela tok bai i givim laip long yu. Dispela tok inap mekim yu i stap gut tru.


## Proverbs 4:23 (id: 10020004023)

23Gutpela tingting bilong stiaim laip bilong yu, em i save kamap long tingting bilong yu, olsem wara i save kamap long hul wara. Olsem na olgeta taim yu mas was gut tru long tingting bilong yu.


## Proverbs 4:24 (id: 10020004024)

24Yu no ken mekim tok giaman na olkain tok nogut.


## Proverbs 4:25 (id: 10020004025)

25Yu mas lukluk stret long rot yu laik i go long en. Yu no ken lukluk nabaut.


## Proverbs 4:26 (id: 10020004026)

26Tingting gut long wokabaut bilong yu, na olgeta pasin bilong yu bai i kamap gutpela.


## Proverbs 4:27 (id: 10020004027)

27Yu no ken lusim gutpela rot yu wokabaut long en. Yu no ken i go long han sut o long han kais. Nogat. Yu mas abrusim olkain pasin nogut.


## Proverbs 5:1 (id: 10020005001)

1Pikinini, putim yau gut long tok bilong mi. Mi laik givim gutpela tingting na save long yu.


## Proverbs 5:2 (id: 10020005002)

2Olsem bai yu inap wokabaut long stretpela pasin, na bai olgeta tok bilong yu i olsem tok bilong man i gat gutpela save.


## Proverbs 5:3 (id: 10020005003)

3Yu mas mekim olsem, long wanem, sapos wanpela meri i lusim man bilong en, na i aigris long yu, orait em bai i mekim planti switpela tok gris bilong pulim yu.


## Proverbs 5:4 (id: 10020005004)

4Na sapos yu bihainim tok bilong en, bihain bai yu pilim em i samting nogut, olsem marasin i gat pait, na em i pen olsem bainat i sutim yu.


## Proverbs 5:5 (id: 10020005005)

5Dispela kain meri bai i bringim yu i go stret long ples bilong ol man i dai pinis.


## Proverbs 5:6 (id: 10020005006)

6Em i no save bihainim stretpela rot bilong laip. Nogat. Em i no save em i bihainim ol rot nogut, olsem na em i go nabaut tasol.


## Proverbs 5:7 (id: 10020005007)

7Orait pikinini, putim yau gut long tok bilong mi. Yu no ken sakim dispela tok.


## Proverbs 5:8 (id: 10020005008)
